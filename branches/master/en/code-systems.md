### Code Systems

This page describes the CodeSystems of the **Medizinisches Forschungsvorhaben** module (naming convention `MII_CS_<Module>_<Name>`). The ValueSets built on them are described on the [Value Sets](value-sets.md) page.

**Important:** CodeSystem resources of external terminologies (e.g. ICD-10-GM, OPS, SNOMED CT) are **not** published in this module; they are obtained from the central KDS terminology service (SU-TermServ): [https://mii-termserv.de/](https://mii-termserv.de/).

> **Written during migration - review before release.** The source's terminology notes are collected on the [ValueSets](value-sets.md) page.
